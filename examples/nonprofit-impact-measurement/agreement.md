## 1. Summary

Riverbend wants to show funders and its board what its network achieves, without adding
work for its pantries. We propose a five-month engagement to choose a small set of
measures, collect them with the tools Riverbend already uses, and hand over a simple,
sustainable process.

## 2. Background

Riverbend coordinates 40 volunteer-run pantries. Each pantry keeps its own sign-in sheets,
and the network reports only pounds of food distributed. Two funders have asked for
evidence of outcomes, such as households served regularly and referrals to other services.

Staff time is the main constraint: Riverbend has three staff, and pantries are run by
volunteers who will not adopt complicated tools.

## 3. Objectives

- **O1. Agree on what to measure.** A short list of measures that funders and the board value and that pantries can collect.
- **O2. Collect it without new burden.** A collection process that fits inside two staff hours a week.
- **O3. Tell the story.** A one-page impact summary that Riverbend can update each quarter.

## 4. Scope

The engagement covers measurement design, a pilot in five pantries, and handoff.

### In scope

- **S1. Current-state review.** Review sign-in sheets, the distribution log, and current funder reports.
- **S2. Measure selection.** Interviews with staff, three pantry leads, and two funders; a scored shortlist.
- **S3. Pilot.** Run the new collection process in five pantries for eight weeks.
- **S4. Handoff.** Written procedures and a training session for staff.

### Out of scope

The following are not part of this engagement unless the parties agree to add them through the change process.

- **X1. Software development.** No custom applications or databases; the work uses Riverbend's existing spreadsheets.
- **X2. Ongoing data collection.** Riverbend staff and pantries collect the data; the team coaches.
- **X3. Grant writing.** The team provides the impact summary, not grant applications.

## 5. Approach

We start from the data Riverbend already has, pilot early, and adjust from what the pilot
shows before handing over.

## 11. Assumptions

This plan assumes the following. If an assumption proves wrong, the parties will review its effect through the change process.

- Pantry sign-in sheets record a household identifier or name.
- The Programs Manager can give about one hour a week to the project.

## 12. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Pantries do not return pilot data on time. | Choose pilot pantries with reliable leads; send a weekly reminder. (Riverbend Food Network) |
| Funders want outcomes that take longer than five months to show. | Present the engagement as building the system and a baseline, not proving impact. (Northfield Volunteer Consultants) |

## 13. Measures of success

- Riverbend approves a measure set of six to eight measures.
- Staff run collection for the pilot pantries within two hours a week.
